# Story Mode – Chapter 1: Rise of the Toilets

## How it plays (built)

- **J** opens the story screen. You can change the key in Controls. Right-clicking the **Alliance Commander**
  opens it too. The Commander can't be hurt, and you place him with his spawn egg.
- Pick an episode and press **Start**. Enemies arrive about 10 blocks away, and allies spawn next to you. The
  tracker in the top-left shows the objective and how far along you are.
- A finished mission turns gold with a "!". **Claim Reward** gives its points and items. Points are spent on powers
  and tech in the Powers menu (**K**); see [powers.md](powers.md).
- A mission **fails** if everyone in it is down at once, if everyone gets about 96 blocks away, or on Peaceful.
  It ends with no penalty if you log out. You can also Abandon it. To retry, press Start again.
- **Parties** (up to 4): `/party invite <player>`, `/party accept`, `/party decline`, `/party leave`,
  `/party kick <player>`, `/party list`. Party members within 64 blocks join the mission, and enemies get 50%
  more health per extra player. Everyone keeps their own story. Only players for whom this is the next
  mission get it marked done; the others "helped".
- G-Man Toilet leaves at half health in every episode before the finale (Ep. 38).
- Admin commands: `/skibidi story complete <players> <mission>` and `/skibidi story reset <players>`.

## Characters added for Chapter 1

- **Skibidi Urinal**: can't move. Spits water up to 14 blocks (2 damage plus Slowness) and bites up close.
- **Micro Skibidi Toilet**: tiny, fast, 6 HP, and sings in a squeaky voice.
- **Flying Skibidi Toilet**: flies, dive-bombs to bite, then pulls back up.
- **Giant Flying Skibidi Toilet** (boss, no egg): 200 HP. Circles about 9 blocks up firing lasers, drops Flying
  Toilets, and swoops down to bite about every 12 s.
- **DJ Skibidi Toilet**: plays a drum beat. Every toilet within 16 blocks gets Speed and Resistance.
- **Saint Skibidi Toilet**: gold halo. Its hymn gives every toilet around it Regeneration. At half health it
  turns demonic: red eyes, no more healing, much faster and stronger.
- **Triple Skibidi Toilet**: three heads, 120 HP. Each bite also catches one extra victim per head. Every
  third of its health lost flushes a head.
- **Fast Armored Skibidi Toilet**: a black armored box with heavy armor. Revs up, then charges in a straight
  line at about 18 blocks a second, doing 12 damage. After a charge its armor is open for 3 s.
- **Hat Skibidi Toilet**: top hat. Leaps at you from 4–14 blocks and slams down, hurting and knocking back
  everyone nearby.
- **Large Cameraman / Large Speakerman** (Alliance): 1.6x size, 60/70 HP. The Speakerman's punches throw
  further.
- **Camera Strider** (Alliance): a camera on three long legs. Shoots toilets, flying ones too, with blue bolts
  from 28 blocks away.
- All of these have spawn eggs. None of them spawn naturally.

## Mission list

One mission per YouTube episode, grouped into the wiki's seasons (these are the tabs in the quest tree).
Each mission unlocks the next one. You start a mission from the story screen or from the Alliance Commander,
and its enemies spawn about 10 blocks away.

- Episodes 1–7 are **survival** missions. You're an Alliance scout who arrived early, and you have to stay alive.
- From episode 8 on, the missions are fights, with Alliance allies spawning next to you.

**NEW** marks a character from the episode that we don't have yet. If we don't add it, the mission uses the
stand-in in brackets.
